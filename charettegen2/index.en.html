We are descended from the ancestor Mathieu Choret through his last-born, Pierre.  He is known as Pierre le cadet, or the younger, to tell him apart from Pierre l'aîné (the elder), one of his brothers.

Pierre le cadet was born in 1662 in Beauport.  At that time New France was in a critical state; its very survival was at stake.  The Iroquois wars were causing financial ruin through their impact on the fur trade, and endangered the safety of the settlers, who had to live in "forts" and patrol the surrounding area under arms, especially in Ville-Marie.  Some left the colony; few came to settle there.

The King of France, Louis XIV, would turn the situation around from 1663 on, so much so that people even speak of a golden age of New France between 1665 and 1673.

So Pierre le cadet was able to spend his childhood in a growing colony and in turn found a family there that would give him many descendants. 

 

A GOVERNMENT, SOME GIRLS AND A REGIMENT

In the early 1660s the young Louis XIV came into his own and decided to exercise his  royal power over France and its colonies.   
For New France, the king and his minister Colbert quickly put in place remedies that ensured the colony's survival and growth.  Louis is the king who did the most for the development of Canada under the French regime.
