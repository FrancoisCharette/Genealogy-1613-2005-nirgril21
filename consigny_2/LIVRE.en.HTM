BOOK OF REMEMBRANCE

 

 THERE ARE CURRENTLY SIX BOOKS OF REMEMBRANCE THAT COMMEMORATE, BY NAME, THE THOUSANDS OF CANADIANS WHO LOST THEIR LIVES IN SUPPORT OF THE CAUSES CANADA DEFENDED IN TIME OF WAR.

THESE BOOKS ARE  DISPLAYED IN THE MEMORIAL CHAMBER OF THE PEACE TOWER ON PARLIAMENT HILL IN OTTAWA.  

ONE PAGE IS TURNED EACH DAY. 

CHARLES'S NAME APPEARS IN THE BOOK OF REMEMBRANCE  COVERING  THE YEAR 1941, PAGE 27.  IT IS  THE FIRST AT THE TOP OF THE LEFT-HAND COLUMN.

IT READS: PTE CONSIGNY,CHARLES  VGC  
that is, Private Consigny,Charles   
Veterans Guard Canada
