FRANCINE'S PHOTOS OF THE PLACES

Francine took her camera and went to photograph rue Létourneux and its back lane in the summer of 2001. To see these photos and the comments, follow the guide...

In 2006, Francine pointed her camera at 580 Bourbonnière, the Côté home at the time of Charles and Ézilda's wedding, and at 2055 Sainte-Catherine, another flat the young couple lived in. To see these photos and the comments, follow the guide...

In October 2009, Francine and I walked various streets of Quebec City to track down places connected with the Consignys and the Thibaudeaus. Since the Saint-Roch district's boom, nothing is left of the houses our ancestors lived in 100 years ago. They have been replaced by the E.N.A.P. (École nationale d'administration publique), apartment buildings or shops. We took these photos all the same, not to mention the ones taken at the Saint-Charles cemetery.  follow the guides...
