The son of Jacques Sanfaçon dit Consigny and Jeanne Bijolet, born in France in the diocese of Langres, François married Marie-Anne Ducharme at Notre-Dame de Québec on November 21, 1735.

François Sanfaçon dit Consigny is said to have been a soldier. The Consignys lived down the river, in St-Jean-Port-Joli, Rivière-Ouelle and St-Pascal, then in Quebec City and Montreal.

Thomas Pageau, born in St-Aignan, Sarthe, France (1642-1706), is said to have come to settle in Canada before 1675, since he married there on November 13, 1675, in Quebec City, a Canadian-born woman, Marie-Catherine Roy (1659-1734).

The Pageaus nearly all lived in Charlesbourg, a municipality north of Quebec City, except for a few generations in Loretteville.  Marie Georgianna Pageau was born in Charlesbourg on July 25, 1865, in St-Charles parish.
 

TWO COUSINS NAMED CHARLES
I put genealogical information about the Consignys on my personal web page. In May 2003, I received an e-mail from a web user named Josée Consigny. Intrigued, she wanted more information about the Consignys, since her grandfather was also named Charles Consigny.  

After an exchange of e-mails, and information she got from her aunt Lucia, we came to see clearly that there were two Charles Consignys born at the end of the 19th century, and that they were cousins.

Here is a chart to explain their relationship better:

Refer to the Consigny family chart higher up on this page:

Nicolas Consigny and Basilisse Landry married on May 2, 1843. They had three children.

  1.  Arsène, married to Amanda Thibodeau; they had three children:

  • CHARLES (February 28, 1893 - October 19, 1941), our grandfather, husband of Ézilda Côté.  They had five children, three of whom reached adulthood: Arsène, Fabiola, Jacqueline.

  • Antoine

  • Crescence

  2.  François-Xavier, married to Lucie Patry; they had three children:

  • CHARLES (August 1886 - June 27, 1943), husband of Dulcina Robitaille; they had seven children (Charles-Eugène, Maurice, Marcel, Jean-Louis, Henri-Paul, Lucia, Robert) (Robert is the father of Josée, the web user)

  • Eugène

  • Yvonne

  3. Nicolas, married to (?); they had four children:

  • Anna

  • Antoinette

  • Jeanne

  • a boy who died at fourteen.
