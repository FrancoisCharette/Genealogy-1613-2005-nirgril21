PHOTOS OF 2055 STE-CATHERINE EAST

Some time after their wedding, Charles and Ézilda moved to 2055 rue Ste-Catherine est. It was torn down around 2004, probably after a fire.

It was the third-floor flat. It faced the big front lawn of the Hochelaga convent, as you can see on this 1912 plan. The lot number is 23-427.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

You can  also see that the lot to the right is vacant and that the two buildings on lots 426 and 427 look identical. So, looking at the photo below, everything suggests that 2055 was part of a row of three houses and must have looked like its two neighbours on the left.

The house at the far left has kept its cornice. These buildings are in poor condition today, but at the beginning of the century they must have looked very fine.

On the vacant lot to the right, a three-unit building went up around 2005; its street addresses are 3693-3697 Ste-Catherine est.
