A series of postcards showing various Kingston sights, of which we have only numbers 1, 3 and 5.  

The date is incomplete (15-41) but refers to 1941, since he was in Kingston at that time; we do not know the month.  Note that Charles is angry with his employer when he writes these cards, and that several words are crossed out and then rewritten.  Perhaps his health was getting worse, since he died in October 1941.

 

Card no. 1

 

Kingston  15-41

Ma petite femme  ma cher Ezilda je te assure que dans le moment je suis pas tres de bonne humeur es majintoi que j’ai demande ma passe et il me long refusée  Apresent ma chere Ezilda je vas la demender de nouveaux

 Kingston 15-41

My little wife, my dear Ézilda, I assure you that right now I am not in a very good mood.  Imagine, I asked for my pass (a leave) and they (the army) refused it.  Now, my dear Ézilda, I am going to ask for it again
