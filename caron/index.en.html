In the ZoneCharette genealogy, our link with the Carons comes through Marie-Anna Caron, the wife of Rosario Charette.
  
She also links us to the Gagnons, through her mother Alphonsine, 
and to the Dionnes, through her grandmother Ombéline. 

In these pages you will find information about the life, the family and the times of Marie-Anna and Rosario.

They also deal with the Caron, Gagnon and Dionne ancestors, 
their history and the lines that link us to them.
 
These three ancestors settled as early as the 17th century in the Bas du Fleuve (the lower St. Lawrence), a region well worth discovering.
