MARIE-</font><font size="4">AN</font><font face="Times New Roman" size="4">NE
                was baptized in Charlesbourg on April 15, 1713. Her godparents were
                Jean Turcot and Marie-Anne Pagé.&nbsp; On November 13, 1730, in
                Saint-Laurent, she married Joseph Bayard, son of Jacques and
                Marie Valade.&nbsp;&nbsp;<br>
                She died on April 2, 1733, in Saint-Laurent. Note
                that she was a widow when she died. A few weeks
                later, on May 28, her fifteen-month-old son Joseph
                died.
@@ 4
ÉLISABETH
                was baptized in Charlesbourg on November
                5, 1714. She married first, on November
                12, 1731, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, François
                Bayard, son of Jacques and Marie Valade. The bride's
                witnesses were Ignace Choret and Michel Beaugis, her uncles,
                and Jean Choret, her brother.&nbsp;&nbsp;<br>
                Élisabeth remarried in the parish of La Visitation de Marie,
                in Sault-au-Récollet, on April 22, 1754, to Joseph-Marie
                Martineau, son of Joseph and Marie-Anne-Geneviève Boucher
                dit Lamirande.<br>
                She died suddenly on October 12, 1798, in
                Sault-au-Récollet.
@@ 5
PIERRE
                </font><font face="Times New Roman" size="4">was baptized in</font><font size="4">
                Charlesbourg on September
                13, 1716. He married Marie Lemay on January 8, 1742, in
                Sault-au-Récollet; she was the daughter of Ignace and
                Marie-Anne Lecompte dit la Gimaudière. Their marriage
                contract was signed on December 27, 1741, before notaire (notary)
                Dauré de Blanzy.&nbsp;<br>
                The couple had seven children. At the baptism of two of them,
                Father Chambon, the curé (parish priest) of Sault-au-Récollet, noted that
                Pierre was absent. The first anno</font></font><font size="4" face="Times New Roman">tation,
                in 1745, reads &quot;<i><b>the father absent at war</b></i>&quot;,
                and the second, in 1750, &quot;<i><b>the father being absent
                in the Pays d'en Haut</b></i>&quot; (the Upper Country).<br>
                Pierre died on October 2, 1755, in Sault-au-Récollet. His widow Marie Lemay married
                secondly Charles Cadot, on February 9, 1756, in Sault-au-Récollet.
                She had other children from this second marriage.
@@ 6
LOUISE
                was baptized in Montreal on March
                13, 1718.&nbsp;Her godparents were Louis Turcot and Marie-Renée
                Duhau.&nbsp;<br>
 On November 26, 1737, in
                Sault-au-Récollet, she married Barthelemy Chaslu dit Chanteloup, son of
                François and Marie-Marthe Fortier.&nbsp; Louise died on
                December 25, 1813, in Sault-au-Récollet.
@@ 7
JOSEPH
                was baptized in Montreal on September 26, 1719. His godparents
                were François Poirier and Marie-Joseph Fayer.<br>
                He married Marie-Anne Lemay on January 8, 1742, in
                Sault-au-Récollet; she was the daughter of Ignace and
                Marie-Anne Lecompte dit la Gimaudière. He died
                on August 29, 1792, in Sault-au-Récollet.

JACQUES
                was baptized on July 23, 1721, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, and
                buried there on December 15 of the same year.
@@ 10
MARIE-JOSEPH
                was baptized on March 10, 1723, in Saint-Laurent. Her godparents
                were Ignace Lemay and Marie Patenote, wife of Charles Rose,
                the child's aunt.<br>
                </font></font><font face="Times New Roman" size="4">She married&nbsp;
                Joseph Dagenais in Sault-au-Récollet on October 24, 1740; he was the son of
                Joseph and Anne Lemay.&nbsp; Joseph, born in 1718, died on
                October 12, 1747. Marie-Joseph remarried, to Joseph
                Chartran, son of Jean-Charles and Madeleine Barbeau dit
                Potvin, on April 14, 1749.
@@ 11
CHARLOTTE
                was baptized on July 19, 1725, in Montreal. Her godparents were
                Michel Baugis and Marieanne Choret.<br>
                </font></font><font face="Times New Roman" size="4">She
                married three times in Sault-au-Récollet and died there on February 6, 1776.&nbsp;&nbsp;<br>
                Her first marriage took place on January 8, 1742, to Joseph Giroux,
                son of Nicolas and Marguerite Blondeau. The bride's witness
                was her uncle Ignace Choret. On June 6, 1746, Charlotte married Guillaume Valade, son of Guillaume
                and Marie-Joseph Deguire. On August 5, 1765, she
                married Pierre Chartran, son of Jean-Charles and Madeleine
                Barbeau dit Potvin.
@@ 12
MICHEL
                was baptized on March 8, 1727, in </font></font><font face="Times New Roman" size="4">Montreal.
@@ 13
PAUL-MARIE
                was baptized on June 29, 1728, in Montreal, and buried on March 25,
                1731, </font></font><font face="Times New Roman" size="4">in Saint-Laurent.
@@ 14
ANGÉLIQUE
                was baptized on April 16, 1730, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, and
                was&nbsp; buried there on September
                24, 1730.
@@ 15
CHARLES
                was baptized on May 25, 1733, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, and
                buried there on August 22
                of the same year.
@@ 16
ANNE
                was baptized on December 12, 1734, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal; her
                godparents were Jean Vanier and Charlotte Rose. She married
                twice in Sault-au-Récollet. The first time, on July
                17, 1752, Anne married Joseph Valade, son of Charles and
                Christine Bertrand. The second time, she married Louis
                Brunet, son of François and Anne Thibault. The bride's
                witnesses were two uncles, Ignace Choret and Louis Turcot. Anne died in Sault-au-Récollet on February 17, 1813.
