In October <b>2009</b>,
        Francine and I walked various streets of Quebec City to
        track down places connected with the Consignys and the Thibaudeaus. Since
        the Saint-Roch district's boom, nothing is left of the houses
        our ancestors lived in 100 years ago. They have been replaced
        by the E.N.A.P. (École nationale d'administration publique), apartment buildings or shops. We
        took these photos all the same, not to mention the ones taken at
