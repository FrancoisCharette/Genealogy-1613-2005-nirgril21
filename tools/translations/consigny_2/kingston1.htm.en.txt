Kingston postcards (1)
@@ 1
A series of postcards showing various Kingston sights,
of which we have only numbers 1, 3 and 5.
@@ 2
The date is incomplete (15-41) but refers to 1941,
since he was in Kingston at that time; we do not know the month.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">&nbsp;
</span>Note that Charles is angry with his employer when he writes
these cards, and that several words are crossed out and then rewritten.&nbsp;
Perhaps his health was getting worse, since he died in
October 1941.
