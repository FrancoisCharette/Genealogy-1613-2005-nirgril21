Where does the name Cap-Saint-Ignace come from?&nbsp;
        The name first referred to a small cape forming a peninsula
        halfway between Montmagny and L'Islet, opposite the present
        parish church.&nbsp; As for the name Saint-Ignace, there is nothing
        definite about it in the archives.&nbsp; The name was probably
        given by the Jesuits, in honour of their founder.&nbsp;
        In their book, the Jesuit Relations, we see that they
        often passed through this place when accompanying the Indigenous people on
        their hunting trips.
@@ 12
The present church dates from 1891.&nbsp;
        It was built between May&nbsp; and October to plans by David
        Ouellet, a Quebec City architect.&nbsp; The outside is of rusticated
        Deschambault stone;&nbsp; the church is 165 feet long by
        64 wide and 40 high.&nbsp; The steeple is 210 feet high, cross
        included.&nbsp; The two-storey sacristy is 50 feet long by 36
        wide and ends in a chapel 12 feet
        in diameter.
@@ 13
Inside, the paintings of the Stations
        of the Cross were painted by an artist from Lévis, Joseph
        Scherrer.&nbsp; He also painted the four pictures in the choir
        recalling various events in the life of Saint Ignatius.
@@ 14
The present organ dates from 1918 and is the work
        of the Casavant firm of St-Hyacinthe; it bears the number 757.&nbsp; It
        has three manuals, thirty-seven speaking stops and a 32-foot
        pipe, not to mention a solid oak case, making it a unique
        and remarkable instrument in the region.
@@ 15
The bells come from the Mears and Steinback
        foundry in England.&nbsp; The big bell is named
        Marie Alice, the middle one Marie Zoé and the smallest Marie
        Joséphine.&nbsp; Not counting the little sacristy bell.
