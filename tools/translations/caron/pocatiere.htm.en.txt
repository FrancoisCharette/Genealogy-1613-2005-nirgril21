@@ 2
In 1678 a parish named <i>La Combe</i> was established on the south shore of the St. Lawrence by
        Monseigneur de Laval.&nbsp; It changed&nbsp; its name to
        Grande Anse, or <i>Sainte-Anne-de-la-Grande-Anse</i>, and kept it
        until 1721.&nbsp;&nbsp; The present name of the municipality,
        created in 1845 (La Pocatière split off from it in 1960), comes
        partly from the seigneury of La Pocatière.<br>
        <br>
        The <i>Pocatière landscape</i> is marked by
        remnants of an ancient Appalachian surface worn ragged by erosion,
        which gives a somewhat jumbled relief.&nbsp; Among other things, this parish has
        a very old school of agriculture (<i>1852</i>) with
        a research station.
