@@ title
Rivière-Ouelle
@@ 2
About ten kilometres
        from La Pocatière,&nbsp; Rivière-Ouelle is a village whose
        main activity is farming, on rich land made up
        mostly of clay.
@@ 3
On October 29, 1672, Intendant Talon granted Jean-Baptiste Deschamps
        De Boishébert, Seigneur de la Bouteillerie, a fief two and a half
        leagues deep along the St. Lawrence River, on each side
        of the Ouelle River. Clearing of the most fertile land
        began right away.
@@ 4
The decree of the King's Council of State of March 1722 mentions
        &quot;La Bouteillerie&quot;, known as Rivière-Ouelle, to designate
        the place that would be set up in 1855 as the parish municipality
        of Notre-Dame-de-Liesse-de-la-Rivière-Ouelle. The people of Rivière-Ouelle
        were nicknamed Capelans and Marsouins (capelin and porpoises) in those days, since they caught
        these fish in abundance.
