A remarkable site on the banks

@@ 3
Saint-Pacôme's charm comes from its
        setting and its topography. Set a little back from the farming
        plain along the St. Lawrence, it clings both to the bank
        of the Rivière-Ouelle and to the side of the marine terrace formed
        a few thousand years ago when the glaciers melted. Salmon
        swim up the winding river into the gorges and
        falls upstream of the village, where anglers come to try their luck
        and where you can go hiking amid
        impressive scenery.
@@ 4
The magnificent estate of the King family,
        who developed the lumber industry and the village's prosperity, bears witness
        to a rich architecture.
@@ 5
A few kilometres from the village, the
        Rivière-Ouelle wharf stands at the end of a bay (the Anse des
        Mercier), in a pleasant spot with a small beach and a breathtaking view
        of the capes of Charlevoix.

FOUNDED
        IN 1851
@@ 7
This municipality, between
        Saint-Philippe-de-Néri and Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pocatière, lies
        about 5 kilometres from La Pocatière.<br>
        <br>
        On February 8, 1851, the parish of
        Saint-Pacôme was canonically established.&nbsp; When it was founded, the
        parish of Saint-Pacôme lived almost entirely from
        logging.&nbsp; While the mills, both the&nbsp; sawmills and the flour
        mill, long provided the people of Saint-Pacôme with a living,
        today the village has turned to recreation and tourism.&nbsp;&nbsp;<br>
        <br>
        A very interesting heritage
        trail takes in nearly 20 houses, the church and a seigneurial
        mill
@@ 8
The church of St-Pacôme was
        built in 1851, with the carpenter Louis Langelier of St-Pascal directing
        the work.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">&nbsp; </span>Barely
        finished, it burned down after the first high mass.<span style="mso-spacerun:
yes">&nbsp; </span>It was rebuilt at once on the same spot,<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">&nbsp;
        </span>and in 1907 it was enlarged with a transept and porticoes with triangular
        pediments in the neoclassical style.
@@ 9
The presbytery,<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">
        </span>built in 1865, is now an inn.
@@ 10
In front of the church,
        the Côte-Norbert leads to the top of a hill (the Côte-des-Chats),
        where a lookout lets you admire and take in an
        exceptional panorama over the whole St. Lawrence plain&nbsp;: the village
        below, the farmland where the river winds down to the
        village of Rivière-Ouelle, then the thirty-kilometre width of the
        river with Île-aux-Coudres and finally, in the distance, the bay of
        Baie-Saint-Paul on the north shore. The light plays with the clouds
        above the river, creating patches of sparkling light in perpetual
        motion.
