From the same census we also learn
        that Jean, the eldest son, aged 17, works as a &quot;<i>chartier</i>&quot; (carter).
        He works 60 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, and earned

MARIE-ANNA
                      AND HER BROTHERS
@@ 16
Jean </b><font size="2">(official name:
                      Joseph)</font>, Marie-Anna's
                      older brother, marries Hélène Vilemaire, daughter
                      of Hormisdas and Catherine Sarault, on May 27, 1919, in
                      St-Zotique parish. They had no children.&nbsp;He
                      worked in printing and lived in Verdun, in a
                      handsome, well-to-do house full of souvenirs of a
                      trip Jean made to Japan and Asia as a young man<font size="2">
                      (André's recollection)</font>. He and his
                      wife take an ocean liner for a trip to
                      Europe in the late 1950s. Jean dies in 1976, and
                      his wife the following year.
@@ 17
Antonio </b><font size="2">(official
                      name: Antoine)</font>, a taxi
                      driver, lived for a long time with a separated woman
                      who had a daughter <font size="2"> (Ghislaine's
                      recollection)</font>. In those days, that way of life&nbsp; made him
                      an outcast from society and from his family. He was&nbsp;
                      allowed to visit his family only alone. There was no
                      question of his bringing his companion. Divorce
                      does not exist, children born out of wedlock have no
                      legal status, and &quot;accotés&quot; (common-law) couples
                      cannot show themselves in public; social convention
                      forbids it. They are a disgrace to their families.
@@ 18
Research in the
                      BMS2000 database tells us that Antoine marries Éva Lamontagne
                      on October 6, 1970, in Sherbrooke. She is surely
                      the common-law partner he had lived with for so long.<br>
                      Éva had first married in Sherbrooke in
                      1921, to George Cross, from England, and had
                      had a daughter with him, Lilianne, who
                      married in 1946 in Montréal. So Antoine and Éva did finally marry,
                      probably when she became a widow;
                      they were each over seventy.
                      Antoine dies in December 1992.

FRIENDS
        FOR LIFE
@@ 22
All her life Marie-Anna
        has good friends; some even appear in the group
        photos at her children's weddings. Her friendship with one of
        them, Imelda Verreault, goes back to childhood. They were both
        born in 1897 and were neighbours, Marie-Anna living at 54 and
        Imelda at 63 rue Delinelle.
@@ 23
Imelda is the first to marry, to a Mr. Henri Philippe. In 1916
        Marie-Anna sends them her best wishes for the new year and
        invites them to her wedding the following month. Mr. Philippe will be the
        accountant of <a href="m_a-ros.htm#garage" target="_blank">Rosario's
        garage</a>, Marie-Anna's husband. Imelda's daughter, Rachel, will marry
        one of Marie-Anna's sons, Rosaire. Later Imelda will live for many
        years at 7078 Des Écores, one of the flats that had once
        belonged to Marie-Anna. Imelda dies in 2002 at the age of 104.
