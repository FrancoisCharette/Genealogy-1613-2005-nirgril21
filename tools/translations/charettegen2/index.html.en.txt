Pierre le cadet Choret — 2nd generation
@@ 1
We
        are descended from the ancestor Mathieu Choret through his last-born,
        Pierre.&nbsp; He is known as Pierre le cadet, or the
        younger, to tell him apart from Pierre l'aîné (the elder), one of his brothers.
@@ 2
Pierre le cadet was born in 1662 in
        Beauport.&nbsp; At that time New France was in a critical
        state; its very survival was at stake.&nbsp; The Iroquois wars
        were causing financial ruin through their impact on the fur
        trade, and endangered the safety of the settlers, who had to live
        in &quot;forts&quot; and patrol the surrounding area under arms,
        especially in Ville-Marie.&nbsp; Some left the colony; few came
        to settle there.
@@ 3
The King of France, Louis XIV, would
        turn the situation around from 1663 on, so much so that people
        even speak of a golden age of New France between 1665 and 1673.
@@ 4

        in a growing colony and in turn found a <a href="famille.htm">family</a>
        there that would give him many <a href="descendance.htm">descendants
