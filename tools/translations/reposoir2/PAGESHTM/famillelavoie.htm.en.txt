IN THE EARLY 20TH CENTURY, ROSE-ANNE CHARETTE AND HER HUSBAND ALDÉRIC&nbsp;
LAVOIE WERE CHOSEN TO TURN THEIR HOUSE INTO A REPOSOIR.
@@ 2
ROSE-ANNE CHARETTE,&nbsp; DAUGHTER OF ALPHONSE CHARETTE SR.
AND ROSIANNE MIRON,&nbsp; WAS THUS THE SISTER&nbsp; OF ALPHONSE CHARETTE
JR., WHOSE WIFE, MARIE LAVOIE,&nbsp; WAS ALDÉRIC LAVOIE'S
SISTER.&nbsp; IN OTHER WORDS, THE CHARETTE BROTHER AND SISTER HAD MARRIED THE LAVOIE SISTER
AND BROTHER.
