@@ 10
Be it known to all that the present
      contract concerning the marriage which, by God's will, shall take place
      before our holy mother the Catholic, Apostolic and Roman Church,
      between Mathieu Chauré, residing in Montréal in New France, son of
      the late Mathieu Chauré and of Jeanne Serre, of the one part, and Bastinne Veillon,
      residing in this town, native of Verdille in Poitou, daughter of Maixent
      Veillon and of the late Bernarde Venet, of the other part.
@@ 11
It having been established before Pierre
      Teuleron, royal notaire, that the parties are of age, and proceeding
      before the witnesses of the groom-to-be, namely François Pierre Rouyer,
      master carpenter, and Robert Hache, his good friends, and Daniel Carteron,
      also a good friend, and, for the bride-to-be, with the consent of
      the Honourable Thomas Venette, merchant of this town, and in the presence
      of other mutual friends of the couple, who, before the assembly,
      promise to take each other as husband and wife, but not before
      the solemnities of the said Church have first been
      observed.&nbsp; The said marriage once consummated and accomplished, the two spouses
      shall hold in common the movable goods they now have and all
      other movable and immovable goods that she or he may acquire during their
      marriage and that they shall have at the end of their marriage.&nbsp; In favour of this
      marriage, the groom-to-be takes his bride-to-be with all her movable
      and immovable goods belonging to her in whatever place,
      ..............
@@ 12
Done at La Rochelle in the
      house of the said Rouyer on the afternoon of February 26, 1647, before Martin
      Darrache and Guillaume Soucy, clerks living in La Rochelle.&nbsp; Both
      parties declared that they did not know how to sign.
