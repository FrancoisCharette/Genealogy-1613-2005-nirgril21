RECEPTION FOR THE FIRST
COMMUNION OF GILLES DAOUST
@@ 2
Gilles Daoust made his First Communion in the
spring of 1962 in St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Lasalle parish, in the Maisonneuve
district of Montréal.&nbsp; His family lived on the ground floor diagonally across
from 2179 Létourneux, the home of Ézilda Côté-Consigny, Gilles's maternal
grandmother.&nbsp; The reception after the church ceremony was held at his grandmother
Ézilda's.
