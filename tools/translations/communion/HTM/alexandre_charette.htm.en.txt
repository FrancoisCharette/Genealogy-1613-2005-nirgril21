ALEXANDRE CHARETTE IS THE YOUNGEST SON OF ALPHONSE CHARETTE
(JR.) AND MARIE LAVOIE.&nbsp; HE LIVED FROM 1901 TO 1924.&nbsp; HE WAS KILLED
IN A CAR ACCIDENT.&nbsp; HE WAS&nbsp; STUDYING PHARMACY AT THE TIME (A BRAND-NEW FACULTY AT THE UNIVERSITÉ DE
MONTRÉAL).
