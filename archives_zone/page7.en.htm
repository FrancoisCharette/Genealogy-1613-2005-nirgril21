MIRON LINEAGE  (paternal lineage of Rosianne Miron, see page 2) 

 Pierre Migneron and   Marie-Anne Plessis, from the Poitiers region of France.

Their son Laurent, born around 1639, came over to New France. He lived in St-Joachim, in the seigneurie of Beaupré, and died there on December 30, 1705.

Laurent Migneron (widower of Anne St-Denis) married Marie Guillaume (a fille du Roi (King's Daughter), widow of Nicolas Maheu) on May 20, 1675, in Québec. The marriage record has not been found; only the contract drawn up before notaire Vachon on May 11, 1675, still exists.
