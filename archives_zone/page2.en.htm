B Joseph-Arthur
The genealogist Brother Benoît calls him Ovila on his B record and Arthur on his M record. In fact, he always signed Joseph-Arthur. He died on March 1, 1968, and is buried in plot R-1539 with his wife, Bernadette Prieur 1883-1945.


B Oscar
  He was baptized Hector, but  changed his first name to Oscar, authorized by the Superior Court on May 2, 1956 (ref.: the genealogist Brother Benoît).  He died on January 12, 1965, and is not buried in plot R-1539. Nor is his wife, Laura Cromp, who died on January 28, 1943, at age 57. 


B Ernest 
The genealogist Brother Benoît calls him Émile on his B record and Ernest on his M record. He died on June 8, 1947, in Verdun and is buried in plot R-1539 with his wife, Yvonne Guay 1892-1979.


B Lumina
(on 13-07-1964 she was buried in plot R-1539, as was her husband, Paul Palardy 1888-1966)


B  Marie (Mary)
The genealogist Brother Benoît calls her Angélina  on her B record and Marie on her M record. She signed Mary. She probably lived in St-Télesphore de Soulanges, where her husband was a farmer according to their M record. We also have photos of relatives visiting her in St-Télesphore. She is not buried in plot R-1539.
B Aldéric
The genealogist Brother Benoît calls him Alexandre on his B record and Aldéric on his M record. He  was buried in plot R-1539 on 20-09-1968, as was his wife, Marie-Louise Houle 1897-1979.


B Irène
Irène never married and took care of her mother. She was buried in plot R-1539 on January 21, 1949.
