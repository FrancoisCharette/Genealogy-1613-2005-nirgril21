Alphonsine Gagnon

Marie-Anna Caron's mother. Born in St-Pacôme, daughter of François Gagnon and Ombéline Dionne; she descends from the Gagnons and Dionnes of the lower St. Lawrence and marries Arthur Caron, a descendant of Robert Caron.

In Nicole's words

Alphonsine Gagnon is born in St-Pacôme, near Kamouraska, on August 26, 1869. She is baptized the same day in the parish of Rivière-Ouelle. Her godfather is Joseph Gagnon and her godmother Léa Pelletier. Her birth record states that her father is a day labourer. Marie-Anna Caron and Rosario Charette — alphonsine
We do not know when, or why, Alphonsine settled in Montréal. Several of her brothers and sisters chose the Chicoutimi area instead. On October 12, 1891, Alphonsine marries Arthur Caron in the parish of St-Charles in Montréal. Marie-Anna Caron and Rosario Charette — alphonsine
Arthur was born on August 16, 1867, in St-Jean-Port-Joli. He was baptized there on the 18th; his godfather was Frédéric Bertin, a sailor, and his godmother Marie Leclerc. His father, Joseph Caron, a day labourer, and his mother, Geneviève Anctil, had married on November 6, 1866, in St-Jean-Port-Joli. They were both widowed, remarrying, and already had children. Arthur's mother had married a Cyrille Avoine in 1852. So Arthur had at least two known half-sisters, Louise and Georgianna Avoine, buried in the Caron plot in Notre-Dame-des-Neiges cemetery.
