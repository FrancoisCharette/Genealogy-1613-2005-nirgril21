In Nicole's words

Pierre le cadet was born on January 21, 1662, in Beauport, on his father's land granted by the Jesuits in their seigneury of Notre-Dame-des-Anges. 2nd generation — Pierre le Cadet (the younger) — enfance
Pierre le cadet Choret married on his birthday, January 21, 1686, at the age of 24. He married Marie-Anne Beaugis, who, like her husband, was born in the colony, in 1665. Their marriage contract was signed before notaire (notary) Paul Vachon on January 19. 2nd generation — Pierre le Cadet (the younger) — famille
From 1688 to 1718, Marie-Anne gave birth to eighteen children. She was over fifty when the last ones were born. Six of them died in early childhood. They were lucky to live in a growing colony, and contributed to it through their work and their large family. But life was not easy for all that. 2nd generation — Pierre le Cadet (the younger) — famille
Pierre died on May 5, 1736, in Beauport; the burial record was signed by Pierre Grenier. He was 74, and with him passed the last member of the first generation of Chorets born in New France. Nearly ninety years had gone by since his parents settled there in 1647 2nd generation — Pierre le Cadet (the younger) — famille
Marie-Anne died on January 23, 1748, in Beauport, at the age of 83; she was surely living with one of her sons or daughters who had stayed in Beauport. 2nd generation — Pierre le Cadet (the younger) — famille
