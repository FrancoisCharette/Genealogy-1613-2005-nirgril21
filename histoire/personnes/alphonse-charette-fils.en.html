Alphonse Charette Jr.

9th generation. A mason and contractor like his father; he marries Marie Lavoie in 1893. Of the couple's nine children only two grow up, including Rosario.

In Nicole's words

Alphonse is born on February 13, 1874, in Saint-Henri, Montréal. He is the eldest son of the large family of Alphonse and Rosianne. His godfather is Charles Charette, his father's eldest brother, and his godmother Théotiste Dubois, his paternal grandmother. All his life Alphonse will follow in his father's footsteps. 8th and 9th generations — The two Alphonses — alph2
On Monday, May 15, 1893, Alphonse Charette Jr. marries Marie Lavoie in Saint-Henri parish in Montréal. The groom's witness is his father Alphonse, and the bride's is her father Olivier. Alphonse and Marie sign their marriage record. Alphonse is nineteen and Marie seventeen. She is a pretty young redhead (Rosario's cousins would later tell Ghislaine that "Marie had red hair 8th and 9th generations — The two Alphonses — alph2
Like his father, Alphonse is a mason. No doubt the son works with the father. When the father retires, Alphonse in turn becomes a masonry contractor. This is first mentioned in the Lovell directory of 1910. He is proud of his work: in the photo opposite he poses, with several members of his family, in front of a stone carved with his name, a kind of artist's signature set on a building under construction. Where is that stone now? 8th and 9th generations — The two Alphonses — alph2
Like his father, Alphonse builds a fine cut-stone house for his family, at 1121 Saint-Antoine. They move in from 1907 and will live there until their deaths. Orphaned, their son Rosario goes on living there during the first years of his marriage. 8th and 9th generations — The two Alphonses — alph2
Marie dies on November 19, 1914, at the age of thirty-nine. For Marie, Alphonse Sr. buys plot R-1539 in the Notre-Dame-des-Neiges cemetery on November 21. She is buried there on November 23. Alphonse Jr. has the first names of his six children who died engraved at the top right of the headstone, even though they are not buried in that plot. He in turn dies on July 9, 1916, at the age of forty-two. A few days earlier he had made his will, in which he leaves all his property to his two underage sons and names as their guardian his brother-in-law and friend, Aldéric Lavoie. 8th and 9th generations — The two Alphonses — alph2
Although nine children were born to them, Alphonse and Marie see only two of them grow up. Most of the others die of infectious diseases, and Marie is said to have caught one of these diseases while nursing them. At least, that is what Rosario would later tell his children when they asked him about their grandmother Marie (Gérard's recollection). Yet no child died in 1914... The 1910 family photo opposite shows only two children. The 1911 census counts no more. What is the real reason for Marie's death? It is well known that children are not always told the truth.... 8th and 9th generations — The two Alphonses — alph2
