Mathieu Choret

The first Charette (Choret) in America. He came from La Rochelle as an engagé of the Jesuits in 1643, returned in 1647 with his wife Sébastienne Veillon and settled near Beauport.

In Nicole's words

Mathieu Choret arrived in New France in 1643 as an engagé of the Jesuits, with a three-year contract. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — engage
Mathieu and Sébastienne marry on Monday, March 4, 1647 , in the parish of Notre-Dame-de-Cougnes in La Rochelle, in the Ste-Marguerite chapel. They marry just before Ash Wednesday, the first day of Lent, a period during which no weddings are celebrated. Easter that year falls on April 21. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — mariage
Mathieu Choret, back from France in 1647 with his new wife, has a one-year contract as an engagé with the Jesuits. When the time comes for him to settle on land of his own, he stays loyal to the Jesuits and chooses their Seigneury of Notre-Dame-des-Anges. This seigneury lies north of the Saint-Charles River and stretches east to Robert Giffard's seigneury (Beauport). Since the land near Quebec was all taken, the Jesuits grant him land at the eastern end of their seigneury, near their farm. The contract is signed in March 1649, and it reads: "three arpents of land frontage on the Great River Saint Lawrence of New France and four leagues deep into the woods, ..." The Moulin brook crosses part of Mathieu's land. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — famille
The Jesuit Relations (Vol. 36, p.114) report that on March 12, 1651, "the house of Mathieu Choret burned down while he and his wife were at Mass in Beauport". Fortunately no one was hurt. The couple has to build a new house, surely a bigger one now that they have been in Canada for four years and have two children. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — famille
Mathieu dies on March 27, 1664, at the Hôtel-Dieu in Quebec. He is buried the next day in its cemetery. Was he taken from Beauport to Quebec and hospitalized because of an accident, an illness, a war wound...? We do not know what caused his death, at about 44. Most of Mathieu's children would also be buried in this cemetery, as would Sébastienne. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — deces
