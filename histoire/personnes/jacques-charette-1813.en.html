In Nicole's words

Three days later, on October 29, 1813, our ancestor Jacques was born in Saint-Laurent, Montréal. He was the second son of Jacques Charette and Marguerite Varin. His godparents were Louis de Carry and Marguerite Hunault. The war would end in 1814, but the country's economy suffered badly from it. Note that in Europe, too, there was war from 1812 to 1814; it would bring about the downfall of the Emperor Napoleon. 7th generation — Jacques — vie
In Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines, Terrebonne county, on November 16, 1835, Jacques married Théotiste Dubois, daughter of Joseph Dubois and Charlotte Papineau. The marriage record states that Jacques is a "farmer, minor son of Jacques Chaurette and Marguerite Varin of this parish" and that Théotiste is the "minor daughter of Joseph Dubois, farmer, and Charlotte Papineau of this parish." So Jacques and his father-in-law had the same occupation: farmer. 7th generation — Jacques — vie
The first child of Jacques and Théotiste was born in April 1837. The following autumn came the Patriotes' Rebellion. Several more children followed, including two sets of twins. The family moved to Saint-Sauveur-des- Monts in 1853, where the couple's last two children would be born. Jacques was a farmer and day labourer. These occupations appear in the baptism records of the couple's many children. Notably, in most of these records the priest added the words "father absent". So it seems Jacques often worked far from home, whatever the season. Let's not forget that at the time a child was baptized very soon after birth, the same day if possible. 7th generation — Jacques — vie
Starting with the 1871-1872 Lovell* directory, we find Jacques and his family in Saint-Henri. His occupation is laborer. In 1871 he was on rue Renaud. From 1872 he lived on rue Saint-Antoine. Note that in those years no house numbers were listed in the directories, only the street name. 7th generation — Jacques — vie
Théotiste died in Saint-Henri on May 21, 1890, and Jacques on December 17, 1897, at the age of 84. 7th generation — Jacques — vie
