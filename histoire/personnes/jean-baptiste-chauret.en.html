In Nicole's words

On March 23, 1749, Jean-Baptiste is born in the Sault-au-Récollet, as his father was before him. It was his grandfather, Pierre, who had left the Quebec area to come and settle on the island of Montréal. 5th generation — Jean-Baptiste — vie
On October 13, 1774, Jean-Baptiste marries Marie-Joseph Lorain, daughter of Paul and Marie-Joseph Rangé, at Rivière-des-Prairies. The registers of the parish of Rivière-des-Prairies are missing for the years 1771 to 1774, so we cannot trace their marriage record. 5th generation — Jean-Baptiste — vie
Eleven children are born of this marriage, at least five of whom reach adulthood. The family spends most of its life in the Sault-au-Récollet, where all the children are baptized except the last, born in 1796. By then the couple had moved to the parish of Saint-Laurent, where Jean-Baptiste dies on June 11, 1798. His wife, Marie Lorain, dies there in turn on April 22, 1807. Her sons Jacques and Basile are witnesses at her burial on April 24. 5th generation — Jean-Baptiste — vie
How is it possible to be related to Marguerite Bourgeoys? Quite simply through marriage, that of Jean-Baptiste Chauret and Marie-Joseph Lorain. Marie-Joseph's ancestors include one of the three nieces Marguerite brought with her to the colony in 1671. So all the couple's descendants inherit Aunt Marguerite. 5th generation — Jean-Baptiste — vie
From all this we can conclude that the couple had eleven children, at least five of whom reached adulthood, four sons and one daughter. The children were all born in the Sault-au-Récollet except the last, at Saint-Laurent de Montréal, a neighbouring parish. Jean-Baptiste died there on June 11, 1798. 5th generation — Jean-Baptiste — descendance
We can assume that a good part of the family moved to Montréal around the turn of the 19th century. Agathe married in 1813 in the parish of Notre-Dame de Montréal. Jean-Baptiste junior and his brother Louis-Amable lived in the parish of Notre-Dame de Montréal, where their children's baptismal records are found. 5th generation — Jean-Baptiste — descendance
