In Nicole's words

Born on October 19, 1921, in Montréal, Gérard is, all his life, a man of his time. He belongs to the generation of men and women who carry out the Quiet Revolution (Révolution tranquille) to give their children a better life and, in Québec, an opening onto the world. Gérard throws himself into the new social movements, often from their very beginnings: the right to education and its importance for everyone, girls as much as boys; the independence movement of the early 1960s; the health trends such as naturopathy, homeopathy, natural foods and therapeutic fasting, etc... Gérard loves driving anything that rolls on four wheels: cars, taxis, trucks, tankers or trailers; he takes part in driving-skill contests and rallies. He loves to keep moving, meet lots of people, belong to groups and associations of every kind, take courses, change jobs, and always have one or two part-time jobs on top of his regular work. Since the latter is often "on shifts", Gérard does not know regular sleep or a regular life, and this for a long time. 11th generation — Gérard Charette
Gérard serves at the end of the Second World War in Europe. Demobilized in the spring of 1946, he marries Jacqueline Consigny on June 29. They have nine children. 11th generation — Gérard Charette
October 19, 1921. Wednesday, around 3 o'clock in the morning, at 444 Sainte-Émélie: birth of Joseph Jean Paul Gérard. His godparents are Jean Caron and Nellie Villemaire, his uncle and aunt. The porteuse is "Mde Z. Laframboise, grand-mère", that is, Alphonsine. He is baptized by Father Potvin at Saint-Zotique church. (source: a little notebook in which Marie-Anna recorded the births) 10th generation — Rosario and Marie-Anna — descendance
Gérard marries Jacqueline Consigny on June 29, 1946. He has recently been demobilized and come back from Europe. The couple moves in with Jacqueline's mother. Gérard drives a taxi, then works for the Post Office, then the City of Montréal, while also doing other odd jobs. After 10 years in Saint-Luc, they come back to Montréal in 1967. 10th generation — Rosario and Marie-Anna — descendance
