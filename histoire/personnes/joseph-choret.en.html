In Nicole's words

Pierre Choret married Angélique Turcot in Charlesbourg on February 1, 1712. Only two of their sons reached adulthood, Pierre and Joseph. The Choret brothers married Lemay sisters. 4th generation — Pierre and Marie Lemay
Joseph Choret Born September 25, 1719, in Notre-Dame de Montréal. Married January 8, 1742, in Sault-au-Récollet to Marie-Anne Lemay Died August 29, 1792, in Sault-au-Récollet, and buried August 30. 4th generation — Pierre and Marie Lemay
We know that in 1743 Joseph lived on the Côte St-Michel and that in 1781 he owned a fully cleared farm of 3 by 20 arpents with a house, barn and stable on the Côte de LaVisitation, and, on the Côte St-Michel, a 5-by-20-arpent lot of standing timber. 4th generation — Pierre and Marie Lemay — vie
