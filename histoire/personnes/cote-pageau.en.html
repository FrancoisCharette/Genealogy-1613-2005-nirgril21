Gaudias Côté and Georgiana Pageau

In Nicole's words

Ézilda's parents, Gaudias Côté and Georgiana Pageau, married on November 6, 1882, in Quebec City, in the church of Saint-Sauveur parish. Gaudias was born on April 12, 1863, in Saint-Jean-Baptiste parish, in the faubourg Saint-Jean in the Upper Town. His parents were Pierre Côté, a blacksmith, and Salomée Pleau. Georgiana was the daughter of a farmer from Charlesbourg, Georges Pageau, and Marie Jacques dit Duhault (photo opposite) . She was born there on July 25, 1865. Neither the spouses nor the witnesses could sign the marriage record. At the Consignys' — FAM_COTE
Gaudias was a shoemaker, then a tradesman; he is said to have had a general store in Quebec City (Uncle Arsène once pointed out to Lucie a spot on boulevard de la Canardière, on the south side, near rue Maufils, as the site of his grandfather's store) . In 1903, Gaudias sold his business, bought a boat, loaded his family and furniture aboard, and sailed to Montreal. He sold the boat and settled at 384 Parc Lafontaine, taking up his trade as a shoemaker again. (Information from Claude Côté, Émilien's son) At the Consignys' — FAM_COTE
In 1910, Gaudias went back into business and became a carter. He signed a contract to haul sugar cane for St. Lawrence Sugar. He bought 15 drays (camions), 24 draft horses and 12 carriages. He ran his business until his death. (Information from Claude Côté, Émilien's son) At the Consignys' — FAM_COTE
Gaudias died on May 14, 1918. He was then living at 299 Bourbonnière (south of Lafontaine), and his trade was carter (ref. Lovell directory). He was fifty-five; the cause of death is not known. Since his widow had no head for business, the company went bankrupt. (Information from Claude Côté, Émilien's son) At the Consignys' — FAM_COTE
Georgianna lived with her youngest children. We can follow her in the Lovell directories until 1947. She moved every year, but always within Maisonneuve. From 1925 she lived at 1699 Jeanne D'Arc, 3rd floor. Then, from 1930 to 1947, at 2179 De LaSalle, 2nd floor. Then she lived for a while with her son Émilien at 2765 Desjardins. From 1949 on, Georgiana was ill and needed a great deal of care. Jeanne, Émilien's wife, with the agreement of the other children, applied to the Aide à la Femme, an institution at the corner of Hochelaga and Aird streets. (Information from Claude Côté, Émilien's son) Georgiana died on August 4, 1951. At the Consignys' — FAM_COTE
Thomas Pageau, born in St-Aignan, Sarthe, France (1642-1706), is said to have come to settle in Canada before 1675, since he married there on November 13, 1675, in Quebec City, a Canadian-born woman, Marie-Catherine Roy (1659-1734). At the Consignys' — arbre_genealogique
The Pageaus nearly all lived in Charlesbourg, a municipality north of Quebec City, except for a few generations in Loretteville. Marie Georgianna Pageau was born in Charlesbourg on July 25, 1865, in St-Charles parish. At the Consignys' — arbre_genealogique
