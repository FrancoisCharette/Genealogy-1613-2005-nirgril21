1616/1626–1698

Sébastienne Veillon

From Poitou, she was living in La Rochelle when she married Mathieu Choret in 1647. Widowed in 1664, she remarried Pierre Aufroy and died in Beauport.

In Nicole's words

The documents do not agree; Sébastienne's birth is placed between 1616 and 1626. But the most likely date is between 1616 and 1618. So it was a woman of about thirty who married Mathieu Choret and sailed with him for New France in 1647. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — sebastienne
On August 25, 1664, in the parish of Notre-Dame de Québec, Sébastienne marries Pierre Aufroy. They had signed a marriage contract the same day before the notaire Paul Vachon. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — sebastienne
Sébastienne dies in Beauport on December 21, 1698. So she had spent the rest of her life in New France, more than fifty years, on the land granted by the Jesuits to Mathieu Choret. 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — sebastienne
He must have had so many things to tell his family and friends! But what seems most urgent to him is getting married. On February 26, 1647, he is before the notaire Pierre Teuleron to sign a marriage contract with a young woman, Sébastienne Veillon 1st generation — Mathieu Choret — mariage
