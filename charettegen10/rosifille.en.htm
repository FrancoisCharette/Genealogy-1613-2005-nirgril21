Photo taken around 1928

It shows Rosianne Miron, widow of Alphonse Charette Sr., surrounded by her five daughters and one of her sons-in-law. 

Top, from left to right, Rose Anne Charette, Aldéric Lavoie (Rose Anna's husband), Rosianne Miron, Alexina Charette, Lumina Charette

Bottom, from left to right, Irène Charette and Mary Charette.
