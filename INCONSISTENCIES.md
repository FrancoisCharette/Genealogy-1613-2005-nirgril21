# Incohérences factuelles / Factual inconsistencies

*FR —* Liste des contradictions repérées pendant la traduction anglaise des sites de Nicole (dates, noms, filiations qui ne concordent pas d'une page à l'autre ou à l'intérieur d'une même page). **Rien n'a été corrigé** : les pages françaises et anglaises reproduisent le texte tel quel. Cette liste sert de référence pour d'éventuelles corrections.

*EN —* Contradictions noticed while translating Nicole's sites into English (dates, names or family links that disagree between pages, or within one page). **Nothing was corrected**: both the French and English pages keep the text as written. Use this list as a reference if you decide to fix anything. General historical errors in the background articles are not listed, only family and internal contradictions. Items quote the French where the wording matters.

1. Rosario Charette & Marie-Anna Caron wedding date: 16 Jan 1917 (2vies, caron/caron.htm lineage table, root index) vs 16 Jan 1916 (charettegen8-9/alph2.htm; communion ROSARIO & Marie-Anna_Caron pages say "1916"). charettegen10/enfance says that in 1916 Marie-Anna invited Imelda to her wedding "the following month" -> supports Jan 1917.
2. Rosario Charette's birth date: 20 Jan 1897 (charettegen8-9/alph2, charettegen10/enfance) vs 29 Jan 1897 (caron/caron.htm lineage table).
3. Arthur Caron's death: 31 Oct 1904 (charettegen10/enfance) -> Marie-Anna was 7; 2vies says "he dies when she is eight".
4. Rose-Anna (Rose Anna) Charette's birth year: 2 Apr 1877 (charettegen8-9/alph1) vs 1878 (charettegen10/rosigr, communion/argentine).
5. Benoît Charette's wedding date: 31 May 1945 (charettegen10/descendance) vs 30 May 1945 (charettegen10/enfance, "réunis le 30 mai 1945 pour le mariage de Benoît").
6. Benoît Charette's birth: 15 Dec 1919 (charettegen10/descendance) vs "(1920-1957)" (communion/BENOIT, charettegen10/rosigr).
7. charettegen10/descendance (Rosaire's baptism, 1923): calls Anastasie Lortie-Lavoie 'l'arrière-grand-mère maternelle de Rosario'; she was Rosario's maternal GRANDmother (mother of Marie Lavoie) and the baby Rosaire's great-grandmother. Translated as written.
8. archives_zone/page2: Marie-Anna Caron "inhumée le 11-07-1666" (obvious typo for 1966; the same page says she died 8 July 1966). Translated as written.
9. archives_zone/page2: Alphonse Sr.'s daughter is spelled both "Émélia" and "Émilia" in the same entry (image file is S_Emilia). The Alexina entry contains a stray fragment "insi que" (likely "ainsi que", rest missing).
10. charettegen6/vie: Jacques born 2 Apr 1781, but the War of 1812 paragraph says he was 'then 35' in 1812 (he would have been 31). Translated as written.
11. charettegen6/cle1: says Jacques Charette was born in 1777; charettegen6/vie and archives_zone say 2 Apr 1781 (archives_zone notes 1777 would follow only from Brother Benoît's ordering, and rejects it). Translated as written.
12. charettegen4: Joseph Choret born 25 Sept 1719 (index, twice) vs 26 Sept 1719 (vie). Also index gives his death as 29 Aug 1792 while noting Brother Benoît's 30 Aug 1793.
13. charettegen4: Marie-Anne Lemay (Joseph's wife) died 7 May 1807 and buried 9 May (vie) vs buried 9 May 1804 (descendance).
14. Rosianne Miron's mother: 'Angèle Picotte' (charettegen7/descendance) vs 'Angèle Pichette' (archives_zone/page7, several times).
15. charettegen7/vie: says the family moved to Saint-Sauveur-des-Monts in 1853 "where the couple's last two children would be born", but charettegen7/descendance lists seven children born in St-Sauveur (1854-1865).
16. charettegen7/vie: the 1881 conclusion names the youngest daughter "Alice, born 1865"; the census line and descendance call her Eliza/Élisa (born 3 Feb 1865).
17. charettegen3/vie.htm: in the 1711 Walker story, "les derniers jours d'août 1709" should clearly be August 1711 (translated as written: "August 1709").
18. charettegen3/vie.htm (seg 23): Marie Rose is said to be born 17 Dec 1666, only six weeks after her parents Noël Rose and Marie Montminy married (7 Nov 1666). Possible, but worth checking the birth or marriage date.
19. charettegen3/cle2.htm: the Grande Recrue text says 105 men landed in Montreal in 1653, but the timeline on the same page says "1653: Arrival of 95 settlers".
20. charettegen3: cle1.htm says the Lower Town church was named Notre-Dame-de-la-Victoire after the Treaty of Ryswick (1697); enfance.htm says it was dedicated under that name in 1690 right after Phips's defeat.
21. charettegen3/index.html: "De 1689 à 1748, soit durant toute la vie de Pierre" but Pierre was born 24 Feb 1690 and died 22 Jul 1749 (vie.htm, enfance.htm).
22. charettegen3/descendance.htm: Pierre and Joseph's mother-in-law is "Marie-Anne Lecompte dite la Gimaudière"; charettegen4 gives "Anne Marie Jeanne Lecompte Lavimodière" (charettegen4 itself notes the spelling varies).
23. (adds to 12) charettegen3/descendance.htm also gives Joseph Choret's baptism as 26 Sept 1719, in Montreal.
24. Angélique Turcot's mother: "Marie Rose" (charettegen3/vie, descendance context) vs "Rose Marie dite Roy" (charettegen2/descendance).
25. charettegen2/descendance: Louis Choret remarried Marie-Josephte Bouthillet on 22 Feb 1745, yet the 1744 Quebec census already lists "Josephte sa femme, 28 ans" (and four daughters aged 4-8, all born during his first marriage to Geneviève Roy).
26. charettegen2/descendance: Jean-Pierre, born 26 Sept 1694 and died 13 Apr 1696, is said to have died "à l'âge de deux ans et demie"; he was about 18 months old.
27. charettegen2/descendance: Hilaire "born 1695" sits between Jean-Pierre (26 Sept 1694) and Jacques (30 July 1696) — possible only if born after about mid-1695; worth checking.
28. charettegen2/descendance: Marie (youngest daughter, born c. 1717 or later) married "le 7 février 1785(?)" — the source itself queries the year.
29. **charettegen2/cle2** — says 770 of the 850 Filles du Roi stayed in the colony, "95%"; 770/850 is about 91%.
30. **charettegen2/famille** — says Marie-Anne bore 18 children 1688–1718 and was "over fifty" at the last births (born c. 1665, so ~53 in 1718); the descendance page's last dated birth is 1716. The 1718 end date / age claim may need checking.
31. **charettegen2/famille** — "Two notarial contracts" (land to Jacques 18 May 1724, to Charles 3 June 1726), whereas the descendance page also mentions land given to Ignace in 1724; possibly three transfers.
32. **consigny_2/arbre_genealogique** — François Sanfaçon dit Consigny's wife is "Marie-Louise Ducharme" in the tree but "Marie-Anne Ducharme" in the text below it (marriage 21 Nov 1735, Notre-Dame de Québec).
33. **consigny_2/index** — Arsène Consigny, born 14 Oct 1865, died 26 Feb 1896 "à l'âge de 31 ans" (also in the quoted 1896 death notice); by those dates he was 30.
34. **consigny_2/2179** — "Ézilda vit plus de quarante ans au 2179 Létourneux", but she moved in in 1925 and died in 1964 (about 39 years).
35. **consigny_2/VIE** — "Ézilda n'a que 50 ans lorsqu'elle devient veuve": born 27 Dec 1891, widowed 19 Oct 1941, so she was 49.
36. **guerre-charles/1914 vs consigny_2/VIE** — 1914 page says Crescence (married 1910) already "a un fils" in Aug 1914 and is expecting another child in November; VIE says the baby baptized on 10 June 1919 (godparents Charles and Ézilda) was Crescence's "premier fils".
37. **guerre-charles/1916** — on 28 Feb 1916 Charles "est soldat depuis plus de vingt mois"; from his August/September 1914 enlistment that is about 18 months.
38. **caron/alphonsine** — Alphonsine Gagnon "naît … le 26 août 1869 … baptisée le même jour" (in Rivière-Ouelle parish), but the caption lower down says "Baptisée le 25 août 1869 à St-Pacôme", the day before her birth.
39. **caron/alphonsine** — Arthur Caron (born 16 Aug 1867) "décède le 31 octobre 1904 à l'âge de 38 ans"; he was 37, which is what caron/enfance says.
40. **caron/gagnon** (family-line chart) — Arthur Caron "Né: 1868, Décédé: 1905", but caron/alphonsine gives born 16 Aug 1867 and died 31 Oct 1904.
41. **caron/gagnon** — Marie Parenteau, "née en 1641", is "âgée d'environ soixante et deux ans" at her death on 17 Nov 1705; that works out to about 64.
42. **caron/dionne** — Antoine Dionne is given as born in 1641 (1641-1721; 26 in the 1667 census), but in 1681 "Antoine a 45 ans" (he would have been about 40).
43. **caron/dionne** — Anne Dionne (born 27 July 1665) "se marie à l'âge de 14 ans, le 25 avril 1679"; she was 13.
44. **caron/dionne** — Marie-Josephte (born 6 Mar 1672) dies "âgée de 64 ans", buried 10 Feb 1736; she was 63.
45. **caron/rosario** (and charettegen10/m_a-ros) — Rosario (born 20 Jan 1897) "décède le 21 mai 1940 à l'âge de 42 ans"; he would have been 43.
46. **caron/rosigr** (photo key) — Benoît Charette "(1920-1957)", but caron/rosario gives his birth as 15 December 1919.
47. **charettegen1/descendance** — Joseph Choret, born 14 Aug 1650, dies 28 Oct 1684 "il a 35 ans"; he would have been 34.
48. **charettegen1/descendance** — Jeanne Choret, born 11 Dec 1652, dies 26 Sep 1718 "à l'âge de 66 ans"; she would have been 65.
49. **charettegen1/descendance** — Jean Choret, born 27 Nov 1657, dies 6 Jun 1699 "il a 42 ans"; he would have been 41.
50. **charettegen1/descendance** (seg 29) — Charles Loignon is called "le frère de sa tante Jeanne, épouse de Joseph Choret", but Joseph's wife is Anne Loignon elsewhere on the same page and in sebastienne.
51. **charettegen1** — Robert Choret born 7 Jun 1648 "à Québec" (descendance) vs "né à Beauport" (famille, which draws a conclusion from the Beauport birth).
52. **charettegen1** — Ignace Choret's baptism: 29 Aug 1655 (descendance) vs "apporté le 27 août" per Lalemant's note quoted in famille.
53. **charettegen1** — famille: "Le cheval n'arrive qu'en 1665" vs voyage: the first horse brought to the country was presented to the Governor on 25 Jun 1647 (Journal des Jésuites).
54. **charettegen5/descendance** — Jacques Chauret, born 2 Apr 1781, "décède le 5 août 1896 ... Il a 88 1/2 ans". 88½ years points to 1869, so 1896 looks like a transposed year.
55. **charettegen5/bigot** — the Le Soleil article says Bigot bought a house at Cressier (near Neuchâtel) and is buried in the church there, while the Dictionary of Canadian Biography excerpt on the same page says he bought a house in the faubourg du Vieux Châtel, Neuchâtel (and died at Neuchâtel). The article also calls Péan "Hugues-Jean-Michel", the DCB excerpt "Michel-Jean-Hugues".
56. **charettegen5** — Jean-Baptiste Chauret's birth: 23 March 1749 (vie) vs "le 10 mai" in the cle1 timeline.
57. **charettegen5/cle1** — "Décès de Pierre, père de Jean-Baptiste Chauret, le 2 octobre 1755 ... à l'âge de 39 ans". But index and vie make Joseph his father (40 years old in 1759, still owning land in 1781) and Pierre his grandfather, who died a few years before his birth in 1749.
58. **charettegen5** — siege of Quebec, 1759: cle1 gives 40,000 English with 150 ships against a town "protégé par 6 000 soldats", while desastre gives Wolfe 8,500 soldiers (49 warships + 119 transports) against Montcalm's 15,000+.
59. **charettegen5/marguerite** — Notre-Dame-de-Bon-Secours chapel: the DCB excerpt has the settlers' work bee to build it in 1657; the museum section says the project dates from 1655 but the chapel was "enfin érigée" only in 1675 (the photo caption also says "chapelle de 1675"); the Le Devoir excerpt says she founded it in 1655 and it was "inaugurée trois ans plus tard" (1658).
