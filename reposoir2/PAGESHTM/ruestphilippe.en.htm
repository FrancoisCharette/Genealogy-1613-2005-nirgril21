REPOSOIR AT 928 ST-PHILIPPE, ST-HENRI, ON SEPTEMBER 9, 1934.   

THIS REPOSOIR WAS PUT UP TO MARK  THE FIFTIETH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LEAGUE OF THE SACRED HEART.  AMONG THE CHILDREN IN COSTUME IS GÉRARD CHARETTE (the 2nd child in the 1st row at the bottom, from the left).

 

SEVERAL PHOTOS OF THIS REPOSOIR WERE TAKEN ON THAT SEPTEMBER 9, 1934;  HERE IS ANOTHER ONE, FOUND ON THE WEBSITE  OF THE SOCIÉTÉ HISTORIQUE DE ST-HENRI.
