DESCENDANTS OF 
PIERRE CHORET AND ANGÉLIQUE TURCOT

(Source: digitized Drouin collection)
MARIE-ANNE was baptized in Charlesbourg on April 15, 1713. Her godparents were Jean Turcot and Marie-Anne Pagé.  On November 13, 1730, in Saint-Laurent, she married Joseph Bayard, son of Jacques and Marie Valade.  
She died on April 2, 1733, in Saint-Laurent. Note that she was a widow when she died. A few weeks later, on May 28, her fifteen-month-old son Joseph died.

ÉLISABETH was baptized in Charlesbourg on November 5, 1714. She married first, on November 12, 1731, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, François Bayard, son of Jacques and Marie Valade. The bride's witnesses were Ignace Choret and Michel Beaugis, her uncles, and Jean Choret, her brother.  
Élisabeth remarried in the parish of La Visitation de Marie, in Sault-au-Récollet, on April 22, 1754, to Joseph-Marie Martineau, son of Joseph and Marie-Anne-Geneviève Boucher dit Lamirande.
She died suddenly on October 12, 1798, in Sault-au-Récollet.

PIERRE was baptized in Charlesbourg on September 13, 1716. He married Marie Lemay on January 8, 1742, in Sault-au-Récollet; she was the daughter of Ignace and Marie-Anne Lecompte dit la Gimaudière. Their marriage contract was signed on December 27, 1741, before notaire (notary) Dauré de Blanzy. 
The couple had seven children. At the baptism of two of them, Father Chambon, the curé (parish priest) of Sault-au-Récollet, noted that Pierre was absent. The first annotation, in 1745, reads "the father absent at war", and the second, in 1750, "the father being absent in the Pays d'en Haut" (the Upper Country).
Pierre died on October 2, 1755, in Sault-au-Récollet. His widow Marie Lemay married secondly Charles Cadot, on February 9, 1756, in Sault-au-Récollet. She had other children from this second marriage.

LOUISE was baptized in Montreal on March 13, 1718. Her godparents were Louis Turcot and Marie-Renée Duhau. 
On November 26, 1737, in Sault-au-Récollet, she married Barthelemy Chaslu dit Chanteloup, son of François and Marie-Marthe Fortier.  Louise died on December 25, 1813, in Sault-au-Récollet. 

JOSEPH was baptized in Montreal on September 26, 1719. His godparents were François Poirier and Marie-Joseph Fayer.
He married Marie-Anne Lemay on January 8, 1742, in Sault-au-Récollet; she was the daughter of Ignace and Marie-Anne Lecompte dit la Gimaudière. He died on August 29, 1792, in Sault-au-Récollet.

It is from Joseph that we are descended. 
For us, he is the fourth generation of 
Chorets in New France.

JACQUES was baptized on July 23, 1721, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, and buried there on December 15 of the same year.

MARIE-JOSEPH was baptized on March 10, 1723, in Saint-Laurent. Her godparents were Ignace Lemay and Marie Patenote, wife of Charles Rose, the child's aunt.
She married  Joseph Dagenais in Sault-au-Récollet on October 24, 1740; he was the son of Joseph and Anne Lemay.  Joseph, born in 1718, died on October 12, 1747. Marie-Joseph remarried, to Joseph Chartran, son of Jean-Charles and Madeleine Barbeau dit Potvin, on April 14, 1749.

CHARLOTTE was baptized on July 19, 1725, in Montreal. Her godparents were Michel Baugis and Marieanne Choret.
She married three times in Sault-au-Récollet and died there on February 6, 1776.  
Her first marriage took place on January 8, 1742, to Joseph Giroux, son of Nicolas and Marguerite Blondeau. The bride's witness was her uncle Ignace Choret. On June 6, 1746, Charlotte married Guillaume Valade, son of Guillaume and Marie-Joseph Deguire. On August 5, 1765, she married Pierre Chartran, son of Jean-Charles and Madeleine Barbeau dit Potvin.  

MICHEL was baptized on March 8, 1727, in Montreal.

PAUL-MARIE was baptized on June 29, 1728, in Montreal, and buried on March 25, 1731, in Saint-Laurent.

ANGÉLIQUE was baptized on April 16, 1730, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, and was  buried there on September 24, 1730.

CHARLES was baptized on May 25, 1733, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal, and buried there on August 22 of the same year.

ANNE was baptized on December 12, 1734, in Saint-Laurent de Montréal; her godparents were Jean Vanier and Charlotte Rose. She married twice in Sault-au-Récollet. The first time, on July 17, 1752, Anne married Joseph Valade, son of Charles and Christine Bertrand. The second time, she married Louis Brunet, son of François and Anne Thibault. The bride's witnesses were two uncles, Ignace Choret and Louis Turcot. Anne died in Sault-au-Récollet on February 17, 1813.

JEAN-LOUIS was baptized on July 16, 1737, in Sault-au-Récollet, and buried there on August 1 of the same year.
