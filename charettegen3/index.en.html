Pierre is the grandson of Mathieu Choret and Sébastienne Veillon, the first ancestors to settle in New France, in 1647.

Come and get to know Pierre and his times.  He lived during the first half of the 18th century; here is what we know of his childhood, his life and his descendants.

A milestone in the history of the Charette line, Pierre is the one who left the Quebec City region and settled on the island of Montreal.


   
From 1689 to 1748, that is, throughout Pierre's whole life, France and England would go to war three times in Europe, but also in their North American colonies. 

Montreal, after a difficult start, became the economic hub of the colony in the 18th century.  Pierre Choret came to settle there with his family around 1720.

The hearth, the bread oven, then the stove: these were all tools that allowed the settlers to feed themselves and keep warm.   And even, in the case of the bread oven, to gain a certain independence.....    Recipes as a bonus!!
